Cloud Solutions

Cloud Solutions

We take a methodical approach: assessing your current infrastructure, designing the right cloud architecture for your needs, and executing the migration with minimal disruption to your operations.

Network Management

Network Management

Your network is the backbone of everything your business does digitally. A slow connection, a misconfigured router, or an unpatched switch can bring productivity to a halt — or worse, leave a door open for attackers. We design and build networks that are fast, stable, and secure from the ground up, whether you're setting up a new office or improving an existing setup that has grown too complex to manage.

After setup, we monitor your network around the clock, catching issues before they impact your team. When something does go wrong, we respond fast — most problems are resolved remotely without any on-site visit needed. We also handle all the security layers: firewalls, VLANs, VPNs, and access controls that keep unauthorized users out.

  • Network Design & Installation
  • 24/7 Performance Monitoring
  • Firewall Setup & Configuration
  • VLAN & Segmentation
  • Wi-Fi Planning & Deployment
  • VPN & Secure Remote Access
  • Bandwidth Management
  • Network Security Hardening
Cybersecurity Services

Cybersecurity Services

Cyberattacks are no longer just a concern for large corporations. Small and medium businesses are targeted constantly, often because they have weaker defences. A single breach can mean stolen customer data, weeks of downtime, financial penalties, and lasting damage to your reputation. The cost of getting hit is always higher than the cost of preventing it.

We take a proactive approach. Our team runs thorough assessments to find vulnerabilities before attackers do, then works with you to close them. We implement the right tools, policies, and training so that your people and systems are prepared. And if something does happen, we have incident response plans in place so the damage is contained quickly.

  • Vulnerability Assessments
  • Penetration Testing
  • 24/7 Threat Monitoring (SOC)
  • Endpoint Protection
  • Security Policy Development
  • Staff Security Awareness Training
  • Regulatory Compliance Support
  • Incident Response Planning

Data Backup & Recovery

Data Backup & Recovery

Data loss is not a hypothetical risk — it happens to businesses of every size, every day. Hardware fails without warning. Ransomware encrypts entire drives in minutes. Employees accidentally delete critical files. Without a proper backup system in place, any of these events can mean days or weeks of downtime, lost revenue, and in some cases, data that is simply gone forever.

We design and implement backup solutions that are automated, monitored, and regularly tested. The test part is crucial — most businesses only discover their backups don't actually work when they need them most. We make sure your recovery plan is verified and documented so that if something goes wrong, your team knows exactly what to do and how quickly you'll be back online.

  • Automated Daily & Incremental Backups
  • Offsite & Cloud-based Storage
  • Encrypted Backup Transfers
  • Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)
  • Regular Recovery Testing
  • Ransomware Protection & Isolation
  • Recovery Time Objective (RTO) Planning
  • Business Continuity Strategy
